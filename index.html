Planted berries start with 2 droplets; a watering fills them to 5. The (+2|+3) is grace. The first plant is a definite (7|8) limit and watering should be before then. Take into account the time it takes you to water/harvest/plant/trade between alts. Good luck farmers and happy harvest!

Berry glossary

Seeds are what one plot needs to plant that berry. Yield is per plot; the planner uses the median for estimates. Drain is the sheet's thirst rating for the berry. Star a berry to pin it to the top of the picker.
