Planted berries start with 2 droplets; a watering fills them to 5. Going even a minute past a deadline loses yield. Once a berry is ready you have 7 hours to pick it up. Watering earlier than the deadline is fine, but it may mean one extra watering, the planner adds it for you.

Berry glossary

Seeds are what one plot needs to plant that berry. Yield is per plot; the planner uses the median for estimates. Drain is the sheet's thirst rating for the berry. Star a berry to pin it to the top of the picker.
